Friend Circles

Pick your side. Mountain or sea, it all grows the same mission.

Join as a friend of Mountain and Sea Foundation. Choose the Mountain Club or the Sea Club - whichever feels like you - and rise through the ranks as your membership continues.

Tiers are set up as recurring gifts on our donation page - pick the club that fits, then choose either the monthly or annual amount at checkout to land in that tier. It's a one-year commitment, not a lifetime one: you're free to renew, change tiers, or step back after that, and always welcome to give more along the way.

The Apex Circle

Mountain and Sea Champion

$50+/mo or $600+/yr

Become a Champion

Mountain Club, Sea Club, and Champion memberships all support the General Fund, which powers all Mountain and Sea Foundation programs and daily operations. You'll receive regular updates on how your donation is put to work.
